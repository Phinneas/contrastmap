The shortlist

Three directions

Ranked by how well each carries both sides of the directory — the cold plunge and the sauna, the studio finder and the shop.

◆ Our pick 01 · Recommended
Cedar & Ice
“cedar for the sauna, ice for the plunge.”

The most literal and the most premium. Cedar is the warm wood every good sauna is lined with; Ice is the plunge. Together they name the whole ritual without saying “therapy.” Reads instantly, sounds like a place you’d want to be a member of, and works as easily on a product page as a studio listing.

Why it wins

  • Exact-match .com is open
  • Carries hot and cold in two words
  • Premium, calm, editorial — fits the serif type

02 · Runner-up
Pine & Plunge
“the friendly, outdoorsy one.”

Alliteration makes it stick. Pine leans into the Nordic, forest-sauna feeling; Plunge is unmistakable. Warmer and more approachable than Cedar & Ice — great if you want the brand to feel like a community and adventure rather than a luxury spa. Its own green-and-glacial palette gives it a distinct, energetic look.

Best for

  • Exact-match .com is open
  • Memorable, playful, word-of-mouth friendly
  • Community / outdoor positioning

03 · The bold play
Thermae
“the original hot-and-cold bath.”

A single, ownable word with two thousand years of story behind it — the Roman thermae paired a hot room and a cold room, the first contrast bathing. Sophisticated, timeless, and roomy enough to grow past cold-and-sauna into a whole bathing-culture brand. The trade-off: the .com is taken, so it launches on a sharp .co / .io.

Consider it if…

  • You want one iconic word, not a compound
  • Premium / editorial positioning
  • You're OK launching on .co (not .com)

Logo direction

One mark, two temperatures

Whichever name you pick, the identity system is the same idea: a single emblem that holds heat and cold at once. The mark splits warm from cool — that is contrast therapy, in one glyph. It doubles as a favicon and app icon at any size.

Before you commit

Next steps

Lock the domain

Confirm and register the pick at a registrar (Cloudflare, Namecheap). Grab defensive TLDs too — the .co/.net and common misspellings.

Trademark screen

Search the USPTO database for conflicts in wellness/recovery classes before you print anything.

Claim the handles

Check Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube for the name. Consistency across handle + domain matters more than the perfect TLD.

Drop it into the site

Once you choose, I'll swap the “Contrast” wordmark on the live prototype for the real name, mark, and palette in a few minutes.

My recommendation: go with Cedar & Ice — it's the rare name that's premium, instantly legible as “sauna + plunge,” and still open on an exact-match .com. Pine & Plunge is the pick if you want warmer and more playful; Thermae if you want one iconic word and don't mind launching on .co.
