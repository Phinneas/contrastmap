**Edge Theory Labs was a real company that sold the Edge Tub, an inflatable cold plunge with a chiller. It went out of business around November 2024, and its own website now says so. Its warranties, including paid three-year plans, can no longer be claimed through the company. If you see an Edge Tub for sale today, it is used or leftover stock with no manufacturer support.**

I'm not issuing a verdict here. Below is the public record, checked against our [Pre-Purchase Scam Checklist](/learn/buyer-protection/scam-checklist), so you can decide for yourself.

## The record at a glance

| Check | What I found |
|---|---|
| Store domain | edgetheorylabs.com. Registered September 2021. The site displays a permanent out-of-business notice. |
| Status | Out of business since around November 2024. The main retailer's product page states that Edge Theory Labs is no longer in business and lists the product as sold out. |
| Warranty (as sold) | 1 year on tub and chiller. Extended plans were sold through third-party administrators (Clyde and XCover). |
| Warranty (now) | No functioning manufacturer claim channel. One owner with a paid three-year plan reported on r/coldplunge that the coverage could no longer be used. |
| Repair options | Third-party repair programs exist, including Inergize Health's "Edge Theory Repair" service. |
| CPSC recalls | None found. |
| ContrastMap Chiller Reliability Index | Edge Tub ELITE: score 45, the lowest in the index. The warranty score is set to near zero because the company is defunct. |

## What owners reported before and after the shutdown

- A reviewer who tested the chiller reported the first unit had multiple problems within 90 days and the replacement broke down within 30 days.
- An r/coldplunge thread titled "Need just a chiller. Edge Theory died" describes an owner sourcing a replacement chiller to keep using the tub. Commenters point to heat-plate clogging as a common failure.
- A YouTube video from around October 2024 notes that remaining stock was being sold "as-is, no warranty."

## A note on "Edge Pro"

Some resale and aggregator listings use the name "Edge Pro." Two independent buying-guide sites say no product by that name was ever sold. The real lineup was the Edge Tub Elite and the Edge Tub Legacy.

## If you already own one

1. Contact the third-party plan administrator directly if you bought an extended plan. Ask in writing whether any coverage survives.
2. Look into third-party repair services, or a replacement chiller compatible with the tub's ports.
3. If you bought within your card's dispute window, ask your card issuer about a dispute for non-performance of the warranty.

## If you're buying used

Assume there is no warranty, and price the purchase as a tub plus a chiller you may need to replace.
