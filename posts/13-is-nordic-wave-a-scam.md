# Is Nordic Wave a Scam? What the Public Record Shows (October 2026)

**Nordic Wave is a real, operating company, Nordic Wave Inc., selling the Viking line of vertical cold plunges from a store domain registered in 2022. I found no BBB profile, no Trustpilot profile, and no recalls. Owner reports on r/coldplunge are split between people who've had no issues and people describing repeated chiller failures. Chiller warranty repairs carry a $250 shipping fee.**

I'm not issuing a verdict here. Below is the public record, checked against our [Pre-Purchase Scam Checklist](/learn/buyer-protection/scam-checklist), so you can decide for yourself.

## What owners report

On r/coldplunge, Nordic Wave threads cut both ways.

- **Positive.** Several owners report a year or more with no chiller issues. One describes a Gen 2 chiller running with zero problems after more than a year.
- **Chiller failures.** A July 2025 thread describes a "1H" error code after about a year, which another commenter identifies as a shorted temperature sensor. The owner objected to the $250 shipping fee for warranty work. Other brand-level threads describe chillers failing within a year, and one commenter reports a neighbor on a fifth chiller in three months.
- **Quick replacements.** Some owners describe Nordic Wave shipping a replacement chiller quickly and without dispute.

Most failure reports don't name a specific Viking model. Treat them as brand-level signal.

## A note on Nordic Wave's own content

Nordic Wave hosts a page on its own site about a competitor, The Cold Life, alleging fake reviews and warranty problems. When you research cold plunge brands, check who published any "exposed" or "scam" page. A competitor's write-up is not an independent source, whichever brand wrote it.

## Before you buy

1. Budget the $250 shipping fee for any chiller warranty claim.
2. Use only Nordic Wave-approved filters. Using others voids the warranty.
3. Follow the winterization steps if you live somewhere that freezes. Freeze damage is excluded.
4. Pay by credit card.
