# Is EverestPod a Scam? What the Public Record Shows (October 2026)

**EverestPod is sold by Everest Labs, a real, operating Australian company. Ice Bath Lab describes it as supported from Melbourne. It sells a budget inflatable ice bath with a built-in chiller. Independent reviews are sharply split, with one Australian review site counting roughly 57% five-star and 40% one-star ratings. The negative reviews center on chiller failures within the 12-month warranty and slow support.**

I'm not issuing a verdict here. Below is the public record, checked against our [Pre-Purchase Scam Checklist](/learn/buyer-protection/scam-checklist), so you can decide for yourself.

## What owners report

Ice Bath Lab's review summarizes the recurring themes in negative verified reports:

- The 90-day return guarantee not honored once the unit has been used. Using it is often the only way to find a fault.
- Week-plus delays in support responses during disputes.
- Difficulty reaching anyone by phone.
- Replacement chillers paid for but not delivered within warranty.
- At least two cases that needed a state consumer affairs office or a lawyer to resolve.

An r/coldplunge owner describes the company as hard to reach, with week-long response times. A separate Facebook post describes a chiller motor failing within two months.

## The bimodal pattern

A 57% five-star and 40% one-star split, with almost nothing in between, usually means the product works well when it works and the support process struggles when it doesn't. That matches what owners describe.

## Your rights in Australia

Australian Consumer Law gives you a right to a repair, replacement, or refund for a faulty product for a reasonable period. That period can run longer than the 12-month warranty. If a dispute stalls, your state's consumer affairs office is the next step.

## Before you buy

1. Test the chiller right away and document any fault in writing.
2. Keep every email. Several disputes were resolved only after escalation.
3. Pay by credit card.
