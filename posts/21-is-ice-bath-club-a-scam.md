# Is Ice Bath Club a Scam? What the Public Record Shows (October 2026)

**Ice Bath Club sells inflatable tubs and chillers online and has a BBB profile listed in Los Angeles. It holds an F rating from the BBB, with 87 complaints filed, and the BBB's own rating notes say the company failed to respond to 86 of them. The most common complaint is a tub that arrives weeks or months before the chiller, or a chiller that never arrives.**

I'm not issuing a verdict here. Below is the public record, checked against our [Pre-Purchase Scam Checklist](/learn/buyer-protection/scam-checklist), so you can decide for yourself.

## The record at a glance

| Check | What I found |
|---|---|
| Store domains | icebathclub.com, registered July 2023 through Alibaba Cloud (HiChina). BBB also lists the business as Icebath.club. |
| Business listing | Icebath.club, Los Angeles, CA (BBB). |
| BBB rating | F. Not BBB accredited. Reasons cited: 87 complaints filed, failure to respond to 86. |
| Warranty contact | The site's return policy directs warranty questions to an email address at qq.com, a Chinese email service. |
| Returns | 14-day cancellation right. Items unsealed for hygiene reasons may be excluded. In some damage cases, the buyer pays to ship the item back. |
| CPSC recalls | None found. |

## What the complaints say

- **Split shipments.** BBB reviews and a widely viewed YouTube review describe the tub arriving first and the chiller arriving weeks later. In one case, the gap was about three months.
- **Chillers that never arrive.** An r/coldplunge thread titled "Ice Bath Club Lawsuit" describes a chiller still undelivered 12 weeks after ordering, with no refund.
- **Chillers that arrive but don't work.** One BBB reviewer says the chiller came three months after the tub and did not function.

Not every buyer reports a failure. A YouTube reviewer who waited about three months for the full order said the system did work once everything arrived.

## How this maps to the checklist

Ice Bath Club hits three checklist flags at once. The first is a near-total non-response rate on BBB complaints. The second is the split shipment pattern. The third is a warranty contact that routes outside the U.S., which makes any dispute harder to pursue. None of these prove intent. Together, they describe a seller where getting a problem fixed may depend on your card issuer rather than the company.

## Before you buy

1. Confirm in writing that the chiller ships with the tub.
2. Pay by credit card, and note your dispute deadline.
3. Know that your warranty contact is an overseas email address.
